01 · The Brief

User Story & Objective

"As a regional/market manager at a Communication Service Provider, I want a simple customer segmentation and personalized offer generator that classifies customers by profile, value, behaviour, usage, lifecycle and contract details — so I can see each customer's segment and the right personalized offer, without needing advanced analytics."

Objective

Build a basic software module that lets the Regional/Market Manager team segment customers and view tailored personalized offers through simple, value-based analysis — then map each offer in the promotions list to the right segment.

02 · Segmentation Logic

Four Lenses on Every Customer

Usage-Based

Data volume, call minutes, texts and app usage — segments that keep each customer on the right-sized plan.

Customer Value

Lifetime value to the CSP shapes the offer: premium support for high-value customers, budget-friendly options for cost-conscious ones.

Bundling Behaviour

Preference for all-in-one internet, TV and phone bundles versus a single service drives tailored bundle offers.

Contract Length

Month-to-month versus annual contracts inform retention strategy — renewal incentives or commitment offers.

03 · Workflow

From Data to Delivery

Each step uses only the customer's own profile, behaviour and contract data — no advanced analytics required to reach a tailored offer.

04 · Inputs & Outputs

Inputs & Outputs

Data Requirements

  • Sample telecom offers and customer profiles, including past feedback and interactions
  • Customer profile data — demographics, preferences, location
  • Behavioural and usage data — service history, feedback, interaction logs
  • Available offers and promotions with eligibility criteria
  • Optional: historical data on offer effectiveness

Expected Output

  • A personalized offer tailored to each customer's profile and behaviour
  • Improved engagement and higher offer conversion rates
  • Dashboards summarizing offer distribution, acceptance and effectiveness by segment
